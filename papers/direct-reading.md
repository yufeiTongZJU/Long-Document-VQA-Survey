## Notation and definitions

**Methods and ordering.** &#42; marks methods primarily evaluated on single pages/images that also support multiple pages. Within task groups, methods follow final conference submission deadlines, then first release within each conference; preprints and journal papers are inserted by first release. In first-release dates (YY.MM), `--` in the month position means unavailable. Venue superscript <sup>F</sup> denotes Findings. `+` denotes combinations, `/` alternatives, and `--` unconfirmed information.

**Parsing and agents.** OCR and Deep are independent dimensions. OCR denotes dependence on external extraction or textualization, including OCR, PDF text extraction, and VLM transcription or description of charts and tables; methods combining extracted text with page images also count as OCR-dependent. Deep = Deep Parsing: semantic overviews or explicit content relations; element transcription or description alone does not count. <img src="../assets/check.svg" width="12" alt="Yes"> denotes presence and <img src="../assets/cross.svg" width="12" alt="No"> absence. Agent distinguishes Single and Multi by runtime reasoning roles and collaboration.

**Model and learning.** Model gives the main answer model or language-backbone family. Learning records added Pretraining, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), or reinforcement learning (RL).

**Evaluation.** Eval. = Evaluation; only dimensions quantitatively reported in the original paper are listed. Ans. denotes answer quality; Ret., retrieval; Loc., localization; Eff., efficiency (parsing, indexing, and online inference costs); and Rob., robustness under visual degradation or interference. Here, Ret. denotes page-level evidence selection or prediction and does **not** imply explicit retrieval in the answering pipeline. Loc. generally measures spatial alignment between predicted and annotated regions.

**Exceptions (<sup>a</sup>).** LayoutLMv3 Loc. is layout-box detection; VinQA (Page Encoding) Ret. is element-citation F1; CoE Loc. includes box-center hits; M3Grounder and DoCoG Loc. include segmentation masks.
